WHO SHOWS UP ON HALLOWEEN?

How weather and darkness shape the trick-or-treat rush: 18 years of half-hour counts from one porch in East Walnut Hills, Cincinnati (ZIP 45207), joined to hourly weather.

• Use the controls to add 2020 back in, recolor the years, or swap the weather on the x-axis.

• Hover over any mark for that night's weather.
